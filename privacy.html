Privacy notice

Last updated 27 November 2026

This notice covers the website arksora.com, run by Arksora Technologies Private Limited (CIN U58202PN2026PTC262931), Pune. It explains what we collect when you ask for a demo and what we do with it.

What we collect

Only what you type into the demo form: your name, phone number, company, city, what your firm makes and any message you add. You give these details by sending the form.

The site has no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking cookies. To see how many people visit and which pages they read, we use Vercel Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, counts visits without recording who you are, and shows us only totals, such as page views, the country, the browser and the site that linked to us. Our hosting service (Vercel) also keeps short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run the site and keep it safe. The fonts load from Google Fonts, which also sees your IP address when the page loads.

Why we use it

Only to contact you about Arksora Works: to fix a demo, answer your questions and follow up on them. We don't sell your details, share them for marketing or add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms passes the form to our email inbox.
  • Our email provider stores the message in that inbox.
  • Vercel hosts the website.

They handle the details only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep it

As long as we are in touch about a demo or about using Arksora Works. If nothing comes of it, we delete your details within 12 months of our last conversation.

Your choices

Write to info@arksora.com at any time to see the details we hold about you, correct them, or have them deleted. We reply within 7 working days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

The Arksora Works app

This notice is about the website only. Firms that use Arksora Works get a separate agreement that covers the data they keep in the app.
